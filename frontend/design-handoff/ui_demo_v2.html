H8

A few things
you might be wondering.

Clear answers, before you start.

What can I upload?

PDF annual reports, Form 10-K filings and financial statements. Guest mode supports one PDF, up to 5 MB.

Can I compare more than one company?

Yes. Select several reports and ask one question: each figure keeps its own source.

How do I check the assistant’s answers?

Click a citation: the original page opens beside the answer with the figure marked.

Is FinHelp a substitute for financial advice?

No. FinHelp is research assistance, not investment advice.
